* Different kinds of blocks
- Built-in definition, single form
- Built-in definition, multiple forms
- Built-in call, single form
- Custom definition, single form
- Custom definition, multiple forms
- Custom call, single form

These different blocks have slightly different needs. Built-ins can talk about
syntax because they teach the language features. Syntax isn't relevant to custom
forms, but the teaching or system role they have is relevant. Likewise,
built-ins are part of a larger, standards spec, whereas custom forms are part of
non-standardized systems.

When reading about builtins, readers want to understand how to write and understand
them.

When reading about customs, readers want to follow logic.

* Built-in, single form

- PACKAGE: <package name>

- Notes
